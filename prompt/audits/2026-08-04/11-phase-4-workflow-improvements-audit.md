# Task 11 Plain-Language Audit — Phase 4 Workflow Improvements

## Result

- Customers can see the photographer assigned to a studio booking, including the photographer’s name, photo, and area of work. The notice names that photographer.
- Freelancers can handle their own booking decisions, including confirming, declining, arriving at the location, and completing the booking.
- Eligible premium studios show a Featured label with a short explanation. Normal search ordering was not changed.
- Customers can add a nearby landmark or directions for an on-location event, and that information appears wherever the booking location is needed.

## What was deliberately not added

The system has no public portfolio page for an individual studio photographer, so no link was invented. Notices remain inside the system; email notices were not added.

Pages displayed correctly, the data change was checked safely, and documentation links and formatting were checked. The local database service was unavailable, so the data change was not applied to that service during the audit.

All requested Phase 4 improvements through 4.5 were recorded as complete, with the stated limits preserved.
