# Task 11 Plain-Language Audit — Phase 4 Workflow Improvements

## Result

- Customers can see the photographer assigned to a studio booking, including their name, photo, and area of work. The notice they receive names that photographer.
- Freelancers handle their own booking decisions directly. They can confirm, decline, mark themselves on-site, and complete the booking without a studio assigning someone else.
- Premium studios that qualify now have a Featured label, with a short explanation of what it means. The normal search order was not changed.
- Customers can add a nearby landmark or directions for an on-location event. That information appears wherever the booking location is needed.

## What was deliberately not added

The system has no public portfolio page for an individual studio photographer, so a link was not shown. Notices stay inside the system; email notices were not added.

## Checks completed

The focused Task 11 checks passed. The complete automated check also passed with 89 tests and 447 checks. The booking-page templates compiled, the new database change was checked safely, and the documentation links and formatting were checked.

The local database service was not running, so the database change was not applied to that service during this audit.

## Conclusion

All requested Phase 4 improvements through 4.5 are recorded as complete, with the limits above clearly documented.
