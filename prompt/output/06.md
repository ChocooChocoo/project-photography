# 06 — Fresh database reset and reseed, preserving users and locations

**Status: done.** A new seeder set under `database/seeders/Fresh/` resets 53 tables, preserves
`tbl_users` and `tbl_locations`, and writes 5 180 rows across 47 tables plus 189 new accounts
appended to the preserved users table — ten studios, ten distinct owners, ten photographers per
owner, and full HR, finance, booking, payroll, procurement, and assistant coverage. No seeded
row contains an image, video, file, media path, media URL, or redirect target. `db:verify-seed`
passes; the suite is 70 green.

---

## 1. Scope

The live schema has 55 tables. They split three ways:

| Group | Count | Tables | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preserved** | 2 | `tbl_users`, `tbl_locations` | Never truncated. Row counts asserted unchanged. |
| **Skipped media** | 3 | `tbl_studio_online_gallery`, `tbl_freelancer_online_gallery`, `tbl_procurement_documents` | Cleared by the reset, then deliberately left empty. |
| **Queue** | 3 | `jobs`, `job_batches`, `failed_jobs` | Cleared, never written — stale payloads would reference deleted ids. |
| **Seeded** | 47 | everything else | Truncated and repopulated with fresh data. |

`tbl_procurement_documents` cannot be seeded media-free at all: `file_name` and `file_path` are
both `NOT NULL`, so a row cannot exist without a file reference. Procurement request states were
chosen accordingly — none of them depends on an attached invoice or receipt.

Four tables dropped by later migrations (`pvt_studio_photographers`, `tbl_online_gallery`,
`tbl_rbac`, `tbl_chatbot_quick_replies`) do not exist and are not referenced anywhere.

## 2. Reset behaviour

`Fresh\FreshResetSeeder` clears the 53 non-preserved tables inside
`Schema::withoutForeignKeyConstraints()`, using `DB::table()->truncate()` rather than a raw
`TRUNCATE TABLE` — the SQLite grammar rewrites it to `delete from` plus a `sqlite_sequence`
reset, so the same code is correct on both drivers.

The table list is an explicit constant, not `SHOW TABLES` minus a preserve list. Introspection
means a drifting preserve list silently wipes something like `migrations`; a constant shows the
blast radius in a diff. A reconciliation pass against `Schema::getTableListing()` *warns* about
any table that exists but is unlisted, and never truncates it — adding a table to the reset has
to be a deliberate edit.

Ordering is children-before-parents even though foreign key checks are off, because SQLite
ignores `PRAGMA foreign_keys = OFF` inside an open transaction. Both subscription plan tables
precede `tbl_subscription_plans`, whose foreign keys are `RESTRICT`.

**Three guards protect the preserved tables:**

1. They are absent from `TRUNCATE_TABLES`.
2. `run()` throws if any name in `PRESERVED` ever appears in `TRUNCATE_TABLES`.
3. Row counts for both tables are captured before the sweep and compared after; a mismatch
   aborts the whole seed.

## 3. Preserved tables

| Table | Before | After | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tbl_users` | 77 | 266 | **+189** (all newly inserted) |
| `tbl_locations` | 23 | 23 | **0** |

No existing user row was deleted, replaced, or modified. One preserved account
(`april.villanueva@gmail.com`, id 76) still carries a `profile_photo` value from before this
change; it is left exactly as found, since editing it would violate the preservation constraint.
Zero fresh-seed accounts carry any media.

`CaviteLocationSeeder` is **not** part of the default chain any more. It deletes non-Cavite rows
and rewrites all 23 LGUs — i.e. it modifies preserved data. `DatabaseSeeder` now runs it only
when `tbl_locations` is empty (bootstrapping a virgin database), and `FreshSeedSeeder` refuses to
run at all against an empty location table rather than writing dangling foreign keys.

**One caveat worth stating plainly.** `tbl_user_roles` is truncated, so preserved users lose
their studio-scoped RBAC assignments. Their `tbl_users` row is untouched — credentials, uuid,
profile, `location_id`, and `role` enum are all identical — but the pivot rows pointed at studios
that no longer exist. Keeping them would have produced dangling foreign keys and failed
`db:verify-seed`. Preserved owner accounts therefore sign in and route correctly through their
middleware, but hold no studio-scoped permissions until a studio is attached to them. This is
the one place where "preserve users" and "reset everything else" genuinely conflict.

## 5. Studio and staffing structure

Ten studios, one per Cavite LGU: Bacoor, Imus, Dasmariñas, General Trias, Silang, Tanza, Kawit,
Carmona, Trece Martires City, Tagaytay City. Each has its own owner — `tbl_studios.user_id` has
10 distinct values across 10 rows.

Each studio carries 14 staff plus its owner:

| Slot | Count | `tbl_users.role` | `user_type` | Scoped RBAC role |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Owner | 1 | `owner` | `Manager` | `owner-super-admin` |
| HR manager | 1 | `studio-hr` | `Manager` | `studio-hr-manager` |
| HR staff | 1 | `studio-hr` | `Staff` | `studio-hr-staff` |
| Finance manager | 1 | `studio-finance` | `Manager` | `studio-finance-manager` |
| Finance staff | 1 | `studio-finance` | `Staff` | `studio-finance-staff` |
| **Photographers** | **10** | `studio-photographer` | `Photographer` | `studio-photographer` |

Photographer titles run from Lead (10 years) down to Assistant (1 year). Each is linked to the
studio through `tbl_studio_photographers` with `owner_id` set to the studio owner and
`specialization` pointing at one of that studio's `tbl_services` rows — the column is a foreign
key into `tbl_services`, not a text label.

Categories rotate across the network with a sliding window (`categories[(studio*3 + i) % 10]`),
so all ten seeded categories are in use and no two studios carry an identical set.

Studio addresses are read back out of `database/data/cavite-locations.php` at seed time rather
than typed by hand, so `db:verify-seed`'s "barangay belongs to its LGU" check cannot drift out
of sync with the address literals.

## 6. Identity conventions

The legacy seeders occupy email sequences 100–599 and the mobile blocks `+63917700xxx` /
`+63917500xxx`. The fresh seed uses ranges neither of them touches:

| Group | Sequences | Example email | Example mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | 4000 | `<name>4000@gmail.com` | `+63918404000` |
| Owners | 4001–4010 | `<name>4001@gmail.com` | `+63918404001` |
| Studio staff | `4100 + studio*20 + k`, k = 1..14 | `<name>4101@gmail.com` | `+63918404101` |
| Clients | 4400–4429 | `<name>4400@gmail.com` | `+63918404400` |
| Freelancers | 4500–4507 | `<name>4500@gmail.com` | `+63918404500` |

Names come from a surname pool deliberately disjoint from every legacy roster, so the dataset
is visibly new even before the sequence suffix. Every account: `status = active`,
`email_verified = true`, `password = Password_123` (the shared `SeedSupport::SEED_PASSWORD`),
`location_id` resolved from a preserved `tbl_locations` row.

`tbl_users` is the one table that is never truncated, so it is the one write that must be an
upsert. `FreshSeedSupport::upsertFreshUser()` keys on email, reuses any existing uuid, and
**refuses** to write when a row with that email already exists whose mobile number falls outside
the `+63918404xxx` block — that would mean the address belongs to a preserved account, and
updating it would breach the preservation constraint. A rerun therefore refreshes the seeder's
own 189 rows in place (`tbl_users` stayed at 266 across two consecutive runs) and never touches
anything else.

## 7. Media exclusion

Every media column is written as an explicit `null` rather than omitted, so the intent shows in
the diff and survives a future migration that adds a default.

| Table | Columns forced to `null` |
|---|---|
| `tbl_users` | `profile_photo`, `cover_photo` |
| `tbl_studios` | `studio_logo`, `business_permit`, `owner_id_document`, `facebook_url`, `instagram_url`, `website_url` |
| `tbl_packages` | `cover_images` |
| `tbl_freelancers` | `brand_logo`, `portfolio_works`, `valid_id`, `facebook_url`, `instagram_url`, `website_url` |
| `tbl_freelancer_packages` | `cover_images` |
| `tbl_employee_attendance` | `check_in_image`, `check_out_image` |
| `tbl_chatbot_configs` | `bot_avatar` |

Three rules that are not obvious from the column names:

- **`online_gallery` is `false`** on both package tables. The gallery tables stay empty, so no
  package may advertise a gallery deliverable it cannot produce. (The legacy
  `MultiStudioBundleSeeder` sets this to `true`.)
- **`tbl_notifications.data` carries scalar ids only** (`{"booking_id": 12}`). That free-form
  JSON blob is exactly where a redirect path would otherwise hide — no `url`, `link`, `route`,
  or `path` keys appear anywhere.
- **`tbl_procurement_requests.invoice_reference` / `payment_reference` are text identifiers**
  (`INV-FS-PR-00001`), not file paths. The invoice document itself would live in
  `tbl_procurement_documents`, which is never written.

A test scans every source file under `database/seeders/Fresh/` for `http://`, `https://`,
`storage/`, `public/`, `asset(`, and ten media file extensions, and fails on any hit.

## 10. Known risks and follow-ups

1. **`migrate:fresh` is already broken — pre-existing, not introduced here.**
   `database/migrations/2026_07_25_120100_drop_fixed_response_chatbot_columns.php` drops
   `fallback_message` from `tbl_chatbot_configs` and `trigger_keywords`, `response_type`,
   `image_url`, `match_count` from `tbl_chatbot_intents`, but the two create migrations were
   edited in place and no longer define those columns. Against a virgin database
   `php artisan migrate` throws at that migration. This is precisely why the reset here is
   truncate-based rather than `migrate:fresh --seed`, and why the new test boots no schema. The
   fix is `Schema::hasColumn()` guards around each `dropColumn`, but that is a separate
   migration-correctness bug with its own blast radius and was left out of this change.

2. **`db:verify-seed` invariants are global.** Its "users not on `@gmail.com`" and "unverified
   users" checks count preserved rows this seeder never touched. The baseline was clean before
   the run, so the passing result after it is attributable; on another database, record the
   baseline first.

3. **Legacy seeders can undo the media-free guarantee.** They are still on disk and still
   runnable by name. Running `MultiStudioBundleSeeder`, `ProcurementWorkflowSeeder`,
   `CoverageGapsAndAdminSeeder`, `FreelancerMarketplaceBundleSeeder`, or `PrismPineStudioSeeder`
   after the fresh seed reintroduces `studio_logo/…`, `procurement/…`, and gallery image paths.
   Mitigated by the `DatabaseSeeder` docblock, not by deletion.

4. **Preserved users are orphaned by design.** See §3 — their role pivots are gone. If any
   preserved owner account needs to work in the owner portal, attach it to a studio and
   re-run `RbacSeeder`, or use one of the ten fresh owner accounts.

5. **Attendance is the only bulk write.** 2 800 rows go in via chunked inserts of 500, as do the
   280 generated payslips and 134 notifications. Row-by-row inserts here were noticeably slow
   over a remote MySQL connection.
