# Handover Notes — booking system

Notes taken at the handover meeting, 21 July 2026. Present: duty manager, head of housekeeping, the developer.

## What we agreed

- **Cancellation window is 24 hours, not 48.** The duty manager said 48 in the written requirements but we agreed in the room that 48 loses us too many late bookings. Twenty-four hours it is. Somebody should update the requirements document.

- **Housekeeping needs to take a room out of service.** A burst pipe in 204 last winter and there was no way to stop the desk selling it. Housekeeping should be able to mark a room out of service, and nobody should be able to book it until it is marked back.

- **Housekeeping cannot see guest names.** Head of housekeeping asked for this specifically. The cleaning list should show room numbers and what state each room is in, and nothing about who is staying.

- **The manager wants an occupancy figure.** How full we were, by week. Nobody said where the number should appear or how far back it should go.

## Left open

- Whether a receptionist can change a booking after check-in. Nobody knew.
- What happens to a booking when the guest never turns up. Currently the desk just leaves it in the diary.
- Whether the 24-hour cancellation charge is taken automatically or added to the bill at the desk. The duty manager thought automatically, the developer said that means taking card details at booking, which we said we were not doing.
