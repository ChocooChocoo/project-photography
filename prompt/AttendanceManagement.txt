Objective #1: Attendance Management along with the Leave Requests

    1). In this process, in the attendance, theres' no special features on that, just a basic attendance management.
    
    2). Since we've alrady implement the leave request, I wanted to change that if the employee has an leave 
        request approved on that day, they cannot use the attendance or time in/out.

    3). In the HR, Finance and Studio Photographer has their own attendance process, but the HR they can see
        all the employees attendance on that studio.

Objective #2: Attendance Management along with the Overtime Requests

    1). Now for the overtime, if the overtime requst is approved, it will take effect on the attendance.

    2). If the overtime is approved, if the employees tries to check out exceeded on their own schedule, the overtime 
        will take effect.

    3). For example, the over requested is 3 hours, then the actual out of the employee is 6PM, if it exceed on 6PM
        it is considered as overtime, so basically 6 + 3 = 9hrs so if the employee exceed on the 9hours or tries
        to check out in 10:30pm, it wont count the extra hours, you know the approved overtime is only 3 hours.
