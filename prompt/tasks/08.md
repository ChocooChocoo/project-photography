# Objective

## Evaluate and Document the Studio Owner Subscription Lifecycle

---

## Role

You are a subscription systems analyst and technical documentation reviewer. Approach this objective with a focus on subscription lifecycle accuracy, platform access behavior, account continuity, and clear documentation.

---

## Description

Investigate the `docs` folder and all relevant subfolders to understand the platform’s documented subscription process for studios managed by studio owners. Evaluate whether the current process accurately reflects a complete and appropriate subscription lifecycle for the platform. Review how free trials expire, what happens to studio-owner accounts and platform access after expiration, and whether the documented behavior is sufficiently clear. Identify improvements based on subscription-management best practices and update only the relevant documentation where changes are warranted.

---

## Primary Objective

Assess the documented studio-owner subscription lifecycle and recommend documentation improvements that establish a clear, correct, and practical process for trials, paid plans, expiration, and post-expiration account handling.

---

## Secondary Objectives

* Determine how subscriptions currently work for studios associated with studio-owner accounts.
* Evaluate whether the existing subscription process is appropriate and complete for the platform.
* Verify whether the system documentation supports free-trial expiration.
* Clarify what should happen to studio-owner accounts, studios, and platform access after a trial or subscription expires.
* Recommend a subscription and expiration process based on established best practices.
* Update the relevant documentation when an improvement is identified.

---

## Success Criteria

* The relevant files in the `docs` folder and its subfolders have been reviewed before conclusions are made.
* The current studio-owner subscription workflow is clearly documented and assessed.
* Any missing, unclear, or incorrect subscription lifecycle behavior is identified.
* Free-trial expiration and its effects on studio-owner accounts are clearly explained.
* Recommended post-expiration account and access behavior is documented.
* Best-practice recommendations are provided for any identified gaps.
* Necessary improvements are reflected in the documentation without modifying the codebase.

---

## Constraints

* Review the `docs` folder and its relevant subfolders before performing the subscription analysis.
* Do not modify the codebase at this stage.
* Limit implementation changes to documentation updates.
* Do not introduce subscription requirements or platform behavior that cannot be supported by the existing documentation or clearly presented as a recommendation.

---

## Out of Scope

* Source-code changes.
* Implementation of subscription, billing, trial, expiration, or account-restriction functionality.
* Changes to the platform’s runtime behavior.

---

## Context & Dependencies

* The analysis depends on the subscription-related information currently available in the `docs` folder and its subfolders.
* The subscription lifecycle applies to studios managed through studio-owner accounts.
* The review must distinguish between currently documented platform behavior and recommended future behavior.

---

## Stakeholders

* Studio owners who subscribe to the platform, begin with a free trial, or experience trial or subscription expiration.
* Studios whose platform availability may depend on the subscription status of their owners.

---

## Supporting Tasks

### Documentation Discovery

* [Tag: Sequential] Inspect the `docs` folder and all relevant subfolders before evaluating the subscription process.
* Identify documentation related to studio ownership, subscriptions, plans, billing, free trials, expiration, account status, studio status, and platform access.
* Consolidate the documented rules into a clear representation of the current subscription lifecycle.

### Current-Process Assessment

* [Tag: Sequential] Determine how subscription plans currently work for studios managed by studio owners.
* Evaluate whether the documented process represents a complete and appropriate subscription lifecycle for the platform.
* Identify inconsistencies, ambiguities, missing lifecycle states, or undocumented transitions.

### Trial and Expiration Review

* Determine whether free trials have a defined expiration process.
* Clarify what occurs when a studio owner’s free trial expires without conversion to a paid plan.
* Determine the documented effects of expiration on the owner’s account, associated studios, platform access, and stored information.
* Identify whether the documentation distinguishes between trial expiration, paid-subscription expiration, cancellation, failed payment, and account deletion when those states are already referenced.

### Best-Practice Recommendations

* Recommend an appropriate process for trial activation, trial expiration, paid-plan activation, renewal, cancellation, failed payment, subscription expiration, access restriction, and reactivation where applicable.
* Define recommended post-expiration behavior without unnecessarily deleting the studio owner’s account or associated information.
* Clearly separate confirmed current behavior from proposed improvements.

### Documentation Updates

* [Tag: Conditional] Update the relevant documentation when the assessment identifies missing, unclear, or inadequate subscription behavior.
* Ensure the revised documentation clearly describes subscription states, lifecycle transitions, expiration consequences, access restrictions, and reactivation expectations.
* Record recommendations that require future codebase changes without implementing those changes.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### Subscription Lifecycle Evaluation

Review whether the documentation defines the complete journey of a studio owner from account creation or trial activation through paid subscription, renewal, cancellation, expiration, and possible reactivation. Determine whether each transition has a clear trigger and a documented effect on the studio owner’s account and associated studios. Highlight any point where the expected behavior cannot be determined from the existing documentation.

### Free-Trial Expiration

Verify whether the platform documentation defines a trial duration, expiration condition, notification process, and conversion path to a paid subscription. Determine what access remains available after the trial expires and whether the owner can still sign in, view account information, select a plan, and reactivate the studios. Where the documentation does not define these outcomes, provide a clearly labeled recommended process.

### Post-Expiration Account Handling

Assess whether subscription expiration affects only paid platform features or the entire studio-owner account. Recommend preserving the account and associated studio information while restricting subscription-dependent functionality, unless the current documentation explicitly requires a different outcome. Clearly document which capabilities remain available and which become unavailable after expiration.

### Recommended Subscription Process

Provide a best-practice lifecycle that distinguishes active trials, active paid subscriptions, expired trials, past-due subscriptions, canceled subscriptions, expired subscriptions, and reactivated subscriptions when these states are relevant to the platform. The recommended process should prioritize transparent status changes, clear owner communication, predictable access restrictions, data continuity, and a straightforward path to subscription reactivation.

### Documentation-Only Scope

Apply approved improvements exclusively to the documentation. Any behavior that is recommended but not demonstrably supported by the current system must be labeled as a proposed requirement or future implementation consideration rather than documented as existing functionality.
