## Enhanced Logic – Location Confirmation Between Photographer & Client

**Core Principle:**  
The confirmation process (photographer marks arrival → client confirms) is **only required** if the booking involves an **On-Location** service delivery.

---

### 1. Booking Type: In-Studio Only (or Flexible + In-Studio chosen)
- **Photographer side:** No option to mark “arrived at location.”
- **Client side:** No confirmation required.
- **Behavior:** The session proceeds without any location-based check-in/check-out steps.
- **Reason:** The studio is a fixed, controlled environment. Arrival is implied by being present at the studio, and no external location verification is needed.

---

### 2. Booking Type: On-Location Only (or Flexible + On-Location chosen)
- **Process remains unchanged and required:**
  - **Step 1 – Photographer marks arrival** at the client’s event/site location.
  - **Step 2 – Client confirms** that the photographer has arrived.
- **Behavior:** Both steps must be completed before the session can officially start (or before time tracking begins, if applicable).
- **Reason:** Ensures accountability, accurate timing, and dispute resolution for travel-based services.

---

## Summary Table – When Confirmation Is Required

| Booking Final Choice | Photographer Marks Arrival? | Client Confirms Arrival? |
|----------------------|-----------------------------|---------------------------|
| In-Studio (only or chosen in flexible) | ❌ No | ❌ No |
| On-Location (only or chosen in flexible) | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
