**Objective: Fix the Online Gallery Display Issue for the Studio Owner and Clients**

1. When the studio photographer or owner uploads images to the online gallery, the uploaded photos do not display properly.

2. When clients open the gallery to view the images, the photos also do not appear. Instead, only the image alt text is shown rather than the actual uploaded images.

**Goal:**
Identify and resolve the issue so that uploaded images display correctly for both the studio owner and clients within the online gallery.
