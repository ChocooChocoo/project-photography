## **Objective: Develop Role-Based Interactive Dashboards**

Design and implement **dynamic, data-driven dashboards** tailored to each user role within the system. Each dashboard should provide **real-time insights**, **intuitive visualizations**, and **actionable metrics** using cards, charts, tables, and filters.

---

### **1. Admin Dashboard**

Create a centralized dashboard for administrators to monitor overall platform performance and user activity.

**Key Features:**

* **User Overview**

  * Total users (segmented by role)
  * Verified vs. unverified studios and freelancers
* **Platform Activity**

  * New registrations over time
  * Active vs. inactive users
* **Revenue Analytics**

  * Total revenue
  * Revenue trends (daily, monthly, yearly)
* **Operational Insights**

  * Booking volume
  * System usage metrics

**Visual Components:**

* KPI cards (Total Users, Revenue, Active Users)
* Line charts (user growth, revenue trends)
* Pie/donut charts (user distribution)
* Bar charts (bookings per period)

---

### **2. Studio Owner Dashboard**

Provide studio owners with a comprehensive view of business performance and operations.

**Key Features:**

* **Employee Management**

  * Total employees
  * Active vs. inactive staff
* **Bookings**

  * Total bookings
  * Completed vs. pending bookings
* **Financial Overview**

  * Income and revenue trends
* **Performance Metrics**

  * Ratings and customer feedback

**Visual Components:**

* KPI cards (Bookings, Revenue, Employees)
* Bar charts (bookings per service)
* Line charts (income trends)
* Rating distribution charts

---

### **3. HR Dashboard**

Enable HR personnel to efficiently manage workforce data and performance.

**Key Features:**

* **Employee Overview**

  * Total employees
  * Department distribution
* **Attendance Tracking**

  * Daily/monthly attendance rates
  * Absenteeism trends
* **Payroll Management**

  * Salary distribution
  * Payroll summaries

**Visual Components:**

* KPI cards (Total Employees, Attendance Rate)
* Heatmaps (attendance patterns)
* Bar charts (department distribution)
* Payroll summary tables

---

### **4. Finance Dashboard**

Provide financial teams with tools to track and analyze financial operations.

**Key Features:**

* **Income Tracking**

  * Revenue streams
  * Profit trends
* **Expenses & Procurement**

  * Procurement costs
  * Expense breakdown
* **Payroll Insights**

  * Total payroll expenses
* **Financial Health**

  * Profit/loss overview

**Visual Components:**

* KPI cards (Revenue, Expenses, Profit)
* Line charts (financial trends)
* Pie charts (expense categories)
* Bar charts (procurement vs. budget)

---

### **5. Studio Photographer Dashboard**

Help photographers manage their assignments and productivity.

**Key Features:**

* **Bookings & Assignments**

  * Assigned jobs
  * Upcoming schedules
* **Status Tracking**

  * Completed, pending, canceled tasks
* **Performance Metrics**

  * Completion rate
  * Ratings/feedback

**Visual Components:**

* KPI cards (Assigned Jobs, Completed Tasks)
* Calendar view (schedule)
* Progress charts (task completion)
* Status breakdown charts

---

## **General Requirements Across All Dashboards**

* Responsive and user-friendly UI
* Real-time or near real-time data updates
* Interactive filters (date range, categories, roles)
* Export options (CSV, PDF)
* Role-based access control
* Clean and consistent design system
