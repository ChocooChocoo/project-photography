# Full Roadmap in Plain Language

## Phase 1 — Fix what was broken

Fix location-name display, gallery images, page alignment, required owner photos, payment confirmation, and the scheduled procurement reminder.

## Phase 2 — Finish partly built work

Add starting prices, studio income reports, a photographer response deadline, a client booking calendar, studio booking cancellation, independent portfolio galleries, automatic expiry of unfinished bookings, broader notices, budget tracking, and review moderation.

## Phase 3 — Core additions

Add package or service selling images, gallery review before customers see photos, a short request-for-changes period after completion, and free-trial settings for subscriptions.

## Phase 4 — Improve everyday workflows

Show photographer details after assignment, simplify freelancer bookings, mark premium studios, allow a written venue address, and explain subscription rank in search results.

## Phase 5 — Larger additions

Support location-based studio discovery, equipment assignment for a booking, repeat bookings, and quality checking for the photo role.

## Phase 6 — Reminders and automatic help

Send reminders for expiring bookings, late gallery uploads, unreviewed studios, and ending subscriptions. Also support payroll triggers and suggestions for suitable photographers.

## Phase 7 — Access checks and broader checking

Make sure people can only open records that belong to them or their studio, and add checks for the most important workflows.

## Phase 8 — Photography help assistant

Replace the old preset reply system with a photography-only assistant, prevent unsafe messages and private-information leaks, keep access keys private, handle failures safely, limit use, and show the assistant in the right parts of the website.

## Phase 9 — Photographer cancellation after payment

Decide and then build a safe response when an assigned photographer cancels: help the owner recover the booking, notify the right people, find a replacement, offer a new date, process refunds or credits, record cancellations, widen the emergency replacement pool, handle cheaper replacements fairly, discourage late cancellation, and consider a backup photographer for high-value bookings.

## Phase 10 — Subscription lifecycle

Decide and then build a complete path for trials, paid plans, ending access, reminder periods, reactivation, repeat billing, cancellation, and plan changes. This phase is blocked until the business choices in [open questions](../00-overview/open-items.md) are made.

The detailed progress record explains which roadmap items have evidence of completion and which remain plans: [progress](../05-progress/detailed-progress.md).
