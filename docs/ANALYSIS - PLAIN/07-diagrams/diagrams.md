# Diagram Guide

The system picture shows the people using the website, the separate work areas for each role, and the shared booking information that connects them. The booking picture follows a customer from choosing a provider through payment. The delivery picture shows photos being prepared and then made available to the customer. The information map shows how people, studios, bookings, payments, assigned photographers, galleries, and reviews are connected.

- **DGM-001 — Booking and payment:** A customer chooses a studio or freelancer, selects a package and schedule, submits the booking, and completes payment before the provider confirms the work.
- **DGM-002 — Studio gallery delivery:** A studio photographer uploads the work, the owner reviews it, and only published photos become visible to the customer.
- **DGM-003 — Freelancer gallery delivery:** A freelancer uploads completed work directly to the customer gallery because there is no separate studio owner review step.
- **DGM-004 — Application structure:** Customers, studio owners, studio employees, freelancers, and administrators use separate work areas connected to the same booking and payment records.
- **DGM-005 — Information flow:** Registration and studio details feed the marketplace; bookings connect customers to providers; payments, assignments, galleries, and reviews extend that booking record.
- **DGM-006 — Main record relationships:** People own studios or act as customers and employees; studios offer services and packages; bookings connect those records to payments, photographers, galleries, and reviews.
- **DGM-007 — Booking and payment conversation:** The website validates availability and booking details, creates a payment session, receives payment confirmation, and updates both the booking and payment records.
