# Thesis Panel Revisions – Studio Booking System

# Objective

## Implement Thesis Panel Revisions and Resolve Reported Defects in the Integrated Studio Booking System

---

## Role

You are a full-stack software developer responsible for an integrated photography studio booking and management system. Approach this objective with the mindset, vocabulary, and priorities of that role.

---

## Description

During the client's thesis defense and mock defense, the panelists requested a set of new features, changes to existing features, and fixes for defects found during testing. The feedback spans the full service lifecycle: onboarding, service listings, budgeting, booking and contracts, photographer and equipment assignment, on-site verification, delivery of photo results, client feedback and reporting, and role-based access control. Several reported defects are rated High or Critical because they allow users to bypass paid subscription limits or role permissions. The goal is to apply every requested revision and fix every reported defect so the system meets the panel's expectations and functions as a truly integrated platform for studios, photographers, freelancers, and clients.

---

## Primary Objective

Implement all panel-requested features and changes, and resolve all reported defects, in the order the feedback was received.

---

## Secondary Objectives

- Enforce subscription plan limits, specifically the staff limit on the Meridian plan.
- Enforce role-based permissions on both the backend API and the user interface.
- Allow studios to sell and deliver hardcopy outputs, not only softcopies.
- Give clients transparency into equipment, materials, and on-site booking progress.
- Give clients ways to give feedback on, and report, studios and photographers.

---

## Success Criteria

- Logging in with valid credentials routes the user to their dashboard without clearing the form.
- "View Users", subscription plan "Update", and "View Freelancer" update/delete actions work without errors.
- The system blocks adding staff accounts beyond the plan's limit (5 on the Meridian plan).
- The barangay list loads when setting up a freelancer profile.
- A photographer who rejected an assignment cannot be selected again for that same assignment.
- Unchecked permissions (e.g., Delete Online Gallery) return 403 Forbidden on the backend and hide the related controls in the UI.
- Checked permissions (Manage, Create, View Online Gallery) grant full functional access in the HR and Studio portals.
- Freelancer access granted for an event is removed automatically once the event ends.

---

## Priority Level

- **High:** Staff limit not enforced. This lets users bypass paid plan restrictions and add unlimited staff.
- **High / Critical (Broken Access Control):** Role permissions misaligned for the HR Manager role.

## Supporting Tasks

*Listed in the order the feedback was received.*

### 1. Initial Panel Suggestions

- **Hardcopy sales:** Allow studios to sell hardcopy outputs through the system. Softcopy-only delivery is not acceptable. Hardcopy orders must offer pickup or third-party delivery.
- **First-login tutorial:** Show a guided tutorial on first login for both business and client accounts.
- **GUI fix:** Fix overlapping text in the panel.
- **Service media:** Allow services to include pictures and multiple videos.
- **Service feedback:** Allow clients to leave feedback on services.
- **User-to-user chat:** Add direct messaging between users.
- **DSS budget recommendation:** Use the Decision Support System to recommend options based on the client's budget range for their target event.
- **Booking duration types:** Support short-term booking (1 day) and long-term booking (2 or more days). Long-term bookings require a contract between the business and the client, with signatures.
- **Downpayment:** Disable the downpayment option.
- **Business image gallery:** Provide a business image gallery that clients can view.
- **Booking preparation:** Show booking preparation time or milestones after payment.
- [Sequential] **Equipment assignment:** After a photographer is assigned, assign camera equipment and other items to the booking.
- **On-site proof:** Require picture proof that the photographer is on-site and the booking is ongoing.
- **Final deliverables:** At the end of the service, the business delivers the photo results as softcopy or hardcopy.
- **Drive link for softcopies:** When uploading softcopies, let the business provide a Drive link so photo resolution is not degraded.

### 2. Client Testing Note (Admin Side)

- **Staff limit:** The staff limit does not appear to work. After subscribing to the Meridian plan (₱27,900/year, 5-staff limit), additional staff accounts created with temporary emails were still accepted.

### 3. Additions from the Mock Defense

- **Camera and equipment specification:** Show the customer which camera brand and equipment will be used at the event, and let the customer choose a preferred camera brand.
- **Materials list:** The studio must list included materials, such as photo frames.
- [Conditional] **Gallery download:** Add a download button to the client's online gallery, available once the owner approves the photographer's request to publish it.
- **Photographer and photo feedback:** Let customers give feedback on the photographer and on the photos.
- **Reporting:** Let customers report either the studio or the photographer.
- **Photo enhancement option:** In each package, let customers choose enhanced photos for an additional price, or raw photos only.
- [Conditional] **Freelancer fallback:** When no photographers are available, let the studio owner hire a freelancer. The freelancer must automatically receive a role and permissions in RBAC, and that access must be revoked right after the event.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Hardcopy Sales and Delivery

Because the system is positioned as integrated, studios must be able to sell physical outputs through the platform rather than only digital files. Every hardcopy order must offer a fulfillment method: either client pickup or delivery through a third-party courier.

### Short-Term vs. Long-Term Booking

Bookings are split by duration. A short-term booking covers a single day. A long-term booking covers two or more days and requires a contract between the business and the client, which both parties must sign.

### Photo Results Delivery

At the end of a service, the business delivers the results as softcopy, hardcopy, or both. For softcopies, the business can share a Drive link instead of uploading directly, so the photos keep their original resolution. Clients can download from the online gallery only after the studio owner approves the photographer's request to publish it.

### Freelancer Access Lifecycle

When no in-house photographer is available, the studio owner can bring in a freelancer.

#### Nested Details

- The freelancer is automatically assigned a role and its permissions in RBAC.
- The freelancer's access is removed automatically right after the event.

### HR Manager Permission Defect

The permission settings configured by the Studio Owner do not match the access the HR Manager actually has.

#### Nested Details

- **Observed:** With "Delete Online Gallery" unchecked, the HR Manager can still trigger and complete a gallery deletion.
- **Observed:** With "Manage Online Gallery", "Create Online Gallery", and "View Online Gallery" checked, the HR Manager still cannot manage or create galleries in the portal.
- **Expected:** Unchecked permissions block the backend API request with 403 Forbidden and hide the delete controls in the UI.
- **Expected:** Checked permissions grant full functional access across the HR and Studio portals.
