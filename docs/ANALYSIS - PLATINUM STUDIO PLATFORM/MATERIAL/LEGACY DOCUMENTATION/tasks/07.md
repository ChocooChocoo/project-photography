# Objective

## Define Contingency Solutions for Photographer-Initiated Booking Cancellations

---

## Role

You are a product strategist and business analyst. Approach this objective by evaluating operational risks, customer impact, booking continuity, and potential resolution policies.

---

## Description

Document the operational issue that occurs when a photographer cancels a client’s booking after the session has already been paid for. Analyze how the platform should protect the client, resolve the paid booking, and minimize disruption to the scheduled session. Explore multiple possible solutions, including photographer substitution, rescheduling, refunds, credits, or other appropriate remedies. Add the findings to the relevant product documentation so the issue can be evaluated before any implementation begins.

---

## Primary Objective

Develop and document multiple solution options for handling photographer-initiated cancellations of fully paid bookings while preserving a smooth and fair client experience.

---

## Secondary Objectives

* Determine how the platform should handle the client’s completed payment when the assigned photographer cancels.
* Evaluate whether a replacement photographer can be assigned to fulfill the original booking.
* Define possible refund, rescheduling, credit, or alternative-resolution options.
* Identify measures that reduce disruption and help the booked session proceed as smoothly as possible.
* Prepare the issue for future product and implementation planning.

---

## Success Criteria

* The cancellation scenario is clearly documented and analyzed.
* Multiple viable resolution options are presented rather than a single proposed solution.
* Each solution explains how the paid booking and scheduled session would be handled.
* Relevant documentation includes roadmap items, analysis notes, and actionable checklist entries.
* The documentation is sufficient to support a later decision about implementation.

---

## Constraints

* Do not implement any changes in the codebase at this stage.
* Limit the current work to documentation, analysis, planning, and solution design.
* Do not assume that substitution, refunds, or other remedies already exist in the platform.

---

## Out of Scope

* Coding or deploying the selected solution.
* Modifying the existing booking or payment workflow.
* Finalizing a single policy before the available solutions have been evaluated.

---

## Context & Dependencies

* The issue occurs within the booking workflow between a client and a photographer.
* The client has already completed payment for the booked session.
* The assigned photographer cancels before the paid session is fulfilled.
* The final resolution may affect booking management, payment handling, photographer availability, rescheduling, and client communication.

---

## Stakeholders

* Clients with fully paid photography bookings.
* Photographers assigned to booked sessions.

---

## Supporting Tasks

### Problem Documentation

* Document the photographer-initiated cancellation scenario and its impact on the client’s paid booking.
* Describe the operational gap created when no predefined cancellation-resolution process exists.
* Add the issue to the appropriate roadmap, checklist, analysis, and planning documents.

### Solution Exploration

* Develop multiple resolution options for handling the cancellation.
* Evaluate photographer substitution as a method of preserving the original session.
* Evaluate full or partial refunds when the booking cannot be fulfilled.
* Evaluate rescheduling with the original photographer or a replacement photographer.
* Evaluate booking credits or alternative compensation when appropriate.
* Consider other resolution mechanisms that could reduce disruption and maintain a smooth client experience.

### Future Planning

* Identify the workflow, policy, payment, and communication considerations associated with each option.
* Record the decisions that must be made before implementation can begin.
* Prepare actionable documentation that can later be converted into product requirements and development tasks.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### Paid Booking Cancellation Scenario

The documentation must address the specific situation in which a photographer cancels a client’s booking after the client has already paid. It should explain what happens to the payment, whether the scheduled session can still proceed, and what obligations the platform has to the affected client.

### Photographer Substitution

Assess whether another qualified and available photographer could be assigned to the booking. The analysis should consider how a substitution would preserve the original schedule, service expectations, and value of the client’s paid booking.

### Refund and Compensation Options

Document possible financial resolutions when the original booking cannot be fulfilled. These options may include a full refund, a partial refund, booking credit, or another clearly defined form of compensation.

### Rescheduling Options

Evaluate whether the booking may be moved to another date with the original photographer or transferred to a replacement photographer. The documentation should explain how rescheduling could minimize inconvenience while maintaining the value of the original booking.

### Client Experience and Session Continuity

Each proposed solution should prioritize transparent communication, fair treatment, and minimal disruption. The analysis should explain how the platform can help the client complete the intended photography session or receive an appropriate alternative when completion is not possible.
