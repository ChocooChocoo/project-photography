# Objective: Fix Booking and Gallery Issues and Enhance Procurement, Refunds, and Revisions

## Goal
Resolve the reported booking, gallery, navigation, and interface defects. Add procurement inventory functionality, update cancellation refund rules, and support photo-specific revision requests.

## Context
The inventory feature under the Procurement Module was previously requested during the mock defense.

## Tasks

### Booking Management
- Prevent users from adding or assigning a photographer after a booking is completed.
- Fix the photographer-facing status so completed bookings no longer display “Waiting for final approval from owner.”
- Correct the priority booking options for 1 day, 2 days, and 3+ days.
- Fix the slot availability counter so it reflects bookings accurately. Reported example: it displays “0/3 available” even when only two clients have booked on the same day.
- Allow the Business Owner to mark a booking as “Complete” when its package or order includes a hard copy.

### Gallery Management
- Prevent photographers and Business Owners from deleting a gallery after its booking is completed.
- Allow clients to select individual photos for revision rather than sending the entire gallery back for revision.

### Profile and Navigation
- Make the right-side profile icon or button open the user’s profile page instead of the Admin Panel.
- Make the freelancer’s “Set up profile” action open profile setup instead of “View profile.”

### User Interface
- Fix the overlap between the X and ✓ action icons.

### Procurement Management
- Add an inventory feature under the Procurement Module.

### Cancellation and Refund Management
- For Business Owner-initiated cancellations, prevent the Admin from customizing refunds.
- For client-initiated cancellations:
  - Automatically forfeit the downpayment and treat it as non-refundable.
  - For fully paid bookings, allow the Business Owner to manually determine the refund amount.

## Open Questions
- What is the intended behavior of each priority booking option: 1 day, 2 days, and 3+ days?
- What inventory capabilities are required under the Procurement Module?
- For a fully paid booking canceled by the client, must the original downpayment remain non-refundable when the Business Owner determines the refund amount?
