# Objective: Plan fixes for client-reported defects and enhancements in a Laravel studio booking system

## Role

Laravel developer able to fix Blade views, CSRF handling, booking workflow states, notifications and payment logic.

## Goal

Write an implementation plan that fixes every defect the client reported and builds the enhancements they asked for.

- Restore the downpayment feature, which has gone missing from the system, and add the cancellation and refund rules that depend on it.
- Fix the two issues the client raised in chat: test notes, and the equipment "Awaiting confirmation" status.

## Success Criteria

- Each defect has a root cause, a code-level fix and a way to verify it.
- Each enhancement has the schema, backend and UI changes it needs.
- The tests that currently fail on the acceptance checklist pass after the fixes: Full payment, Preparation and equipment, Printed photos: pickup and delivery, Finishing a booking, and Full walkthrough: freelancer booking.

## Context

**Client chat (Tagalog, translated):**

- "Can't add notes when something doesn't work." On the acceptance-test page, choosing "Doesn't work" gives no way to explain what went wrong. That page also says "You can only view this page, so your answer wasn't saved."
- "Who are we waiting on to confirm here? Even after the booking was completed, it still only said awaiting confirmation." In the screenshot, the equipment section shows "Preferred camera brand: Canon" but the assigned equipment is "Camera MARK VI (DSLR), Brand: NIKON. Awaiting confirmation."

**Acceptance status:** 22 of 23 tests done, 17 pass and 5 fail. "Photos, videos and extras on packages" has not been tested yet. Its steps already mention an extra charge for edited photos.

**Reported defects:**

1. **Delivery starts too early.** Printed-photo delivery can be started or marked as dispatched before the event is "Completed". The workflow does not check the booking's status first.
2. **No notification for galleries awaiting approval.** When a new online gallery is uploaded and waits for approval, the studio owner's notification badge does not update.
3. **Pickup and delivery settings overwrite each other.** If the owner sets a third-party courier, it overrides or clashes with the client's "Studio Pickup" choice, and the reverse also happens. No rule says whose choice wins.
4. **Employees page crashes.** Opening `/owner/view/employee` gives an HTTP 500 error (ParseError or ViewException: syntax error, unexpected "="). The error comes from `markdown.blade.php` or a compiled view.
5. **"CSRF token mismatch" error.** Changing a booking's status in the management modal (for example to "In Progress" or "Cancelled") fails with this error. Likely causes are a missing meta tag, a missing `@csrf` in the modal form, or an expired session.

**Requested enhancements:**

1. **Equipment inventory.** Replace free-text equipment fields with dropdowns filled from the studio's inventory. Ask clients for their preferred brand and equipment at booking, and track which equipment is available.
2. **Photo editing option.** Let clients choose editing services (color grading, brightness and contrast, retouching) as well as unedited (RAW) photos. The price should update automatically, and photographers' tasks and the scheduling logic should include it.
3. **Rescheduling requests.** Clients can ask for a new date or time. Owners can approve it, reject it, or offer other dates. The system checks photographer and studio availability, and alternative dates are sent to the client's notifications on both web and mobile.
4. **Downpayment settings.** Owners can turn downpayments on or off and set them as a percentage or a fixed amount (`is_downpayment_required`, `downpayment_rate` / `downpayment_amount`). The downpayment is calculated at checkout and shown in the order summary and billing.
5. **Cancellation and refund rules.** If the client paid only the downpayment, it is non-refundable and the studio keeps it. If the client paid in full, the owner enters a full or partial refund amount. Each refund is recorded for audit, based on the booking's `payment_status`.

## Tasks

### Defects

- Add a notes field to "Doesn't work" results on the acceptance-test page.
- Decide who confirms assigned equipment, and make the status change from "Awaiting confirmation". Flag it when the assigned brand differs from the client's preference.
- Block delivery and dispatch actions until the event is "Completed".
- Send a notification to the owner when a gallery is submitted for approval.
- Set a clear rule for whose pickup or delivery choice wins, and stop one from silently overwriting the other.
- Fix the Blade syntax error and clear the compiled views.
- Fix CSRF token handling in the status-change modal and its AJAX request.

### Enhancements

- Equipment inventory module (enhancement 1)
- Photo editing selection and pricing (enhancement 2)
- Rescheduling workflow (enhancement 3)
- Downpayment settings, then cancellation and refund rules, which depend on them (enhancements 4 and 5)
